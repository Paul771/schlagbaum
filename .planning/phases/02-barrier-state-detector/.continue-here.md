<decisions_made>

- **Both failures were fixture defects, not detector-logic problems** — `barrier.py` was behaving correctly in both cases by refusing to guess. `src/detect/` was left untouched.
- **Failure 1 root cause: the arm vanished at night.** `ARM_COLOR` was a fixed grey ≈60 luminance, indistinguishable from a 45-luminance night background, so the arm produced no edges and the night bucket stopped discriminating: measured night/open gap **0.007 vs margin 0.02**. Fixed with `ink_for(brightness)`, which keeps ~100 levels of contrast in both buckets (`cv2.Canny(60,160)` needs a ≥60 step to emit an edge at all).
- **Failure 2 was a wrong test premise, not a thin fixture.** The plan asks for a frame *far from both references*; `sigma=90` Gaussian noise is not one — measured `d_closed` moved only 0.000 → 0.017 while `d_open` held at 0.044, so the frame was recognisably CLOSED. The old diagnosis ("strengthen the fixture") could not have worked, because a MORE discriminative fixture raises the noise gap further. Split into: unrecognisable pixels → UNKNOWN, and heavy noise → stays CLOSED (a BARRIER-04 property).
- **Fixture changes that produced the win:** brightness-derived ink + horizontal background stripes + both ROIs narrowed onto the swept band (y 245..420). Result: worst clean gap **0.0779 = 3.9x margin** (was 1.9x), day/night agree to 0.0002.
- **Vertical stripes were tried and REJECTED:** they merge with the raised arm into a full-height connected component inside the gate band and `_geometry_looks_open` then reads a *closed* frame as raised. Texture must be horizontal. Recorded in the test module docstring.
- **A vacuity guard was added to the day/night bucket test.** Night mean brightness is 56.8 against the 60.0 threshold — only ~3 levels of headroom; had it crossed, the day/night comparison would have compared day with day and still passed.
- **`margin=0.02` is explicitly provisional.** Per `02-RESEARCH.md`, margin and dwell are calibrated against real recordings in plan 02-02. It was NOT raised to make synthetic tests pass, and the separation test still asserts `gap > margin` rather than a hard `3 * margin` so it does not fight that future recalibration.
- **Generator bug found earlier and still relevant (remember it):** `np.linspace(0, 20, 720)[:, None]` has shape `(720, 1)`, so adding it to a scalar produced a **1-pixel-wide** frame and Canny found zero edges everywhere. Fix: `np.full((720, 1280), brightness) + gradient`.
- **A test expectation was corrected, not the code:** an unknown camera returns `UNKNOWN / no_reference_for_bucket` rather than raising — that is the intended "never guess" behaviour.

### Night session (2026-10-06) — measured / observed

- **Night IR-lit frames measure gray mean ~100-130 — ABOVE `bucket_threshold=60`, so they land in the DAY bucket.** The real day/night bucket crossing is the dusk IR switch, not wall-clock. Margin/threshold calibration must respect this; record in `02-02-SUMMARY.md`.
- **Stripe-band baselines are per-camera.** Closed band means: cam_1 ≈128-130, cam_2 ≈101-103 — a shared threshold (e.g. 120) would misread cam_2. Exposure flicker of ±10-28 levels happens WITHIN the closed state, so band means are a labelling aid only; the visual frame read is the label arbiter.
- **Start-of-session stale burst:** the first ~4 frames per camera arrive within ~0.17 s carrying a stale OSD timestamp — they are duplicate copies of ONE camera moment. Reference builders and validators must count unique camera moments, not raw frames.
- **OSD clock drifts 2-12 s behind PC time, inconsistently** — filename PC timestamps (`HHMMSSmmm`) are the ordering ground truth; OSD is for content reading only.
